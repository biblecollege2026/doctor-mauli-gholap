About the doctor

A practice built around the whole person.

Questions

Before your visit.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the form on this page, or call +91 78754 59990. A request becomes an appointment once the clinic confirms it with you.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Any previous reports and prescriptions, a list of medicines you take now, and any questions you would like to ask.

Can I have a consultation by phone?

You can choose a phone consultation when booking. Whether it is suitable for your concern is for the doctor to decide, so please call the clinic if you are unsure.

Can I change or cancel my appointment?

Yes. Please call the clinic as early as you can and quote your reference number, so the time can be offered to someone else.

Is this website for emergencies?

No. In an emergency, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest hospital.
